# ScanEdit Pro — Mac app downloads

ScanEdit Pro lets you edit the text of scanned PDFs and scanned documents. OCR in English, Italian,
Spanish and French runs on your device: your files are never uploaded.

This repository only hosts the installers of the ScanEdit Pro Mac app (Apple Silicon, M1 or later).
It contains no source code.

The app requires a ScanEdit Pro Mac license. Buy one at https://www.scaneditpro.com, then enter it
in the app with 🔑 PRO License.

Website: https://www.scaneditpro.com · Support: support@scaneditpro.com

## First launch on macOS
The app is not yet notarized by Apple, so macOS may say it can't verify it. Open
System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to
ScanEdit Pro, then confirm. You only need to do this once.

## Primo avvio su macOS
L'app non è ancora notarizzata da Apple, quindi macOS potrebbe dire che non riesce a
verificarla. Apri Impostazioni di Sistema → Privacy e sicurezza, scorri in basso e fai
clic su "Apri comunque" accanto a ScanEdit Pro, poi conferma. Serve solo la prima volta.
